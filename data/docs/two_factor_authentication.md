# Two-Factor Authentication

## Setting up 2FA

Go to Settings then Security then Two-Factor Authentication and scan
the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or
Authy. Enter the 6-digit code shown in the app to confirm setup.

## Backup codes

After enabling 2FA, you are shown 10 single-use backup codes. Store
these somewhere safe — each code can be used once if you lose access
to your authenticator app.

## Losing access to your authenticator app

If you lose your device and have no backup codes, contact support
with proof of account ownership. Account recovery in this case can
take up to 48 hours for security review.

## 2FA and SSO accounts

Organizations using enterprise SSO manage two-factor authentication
through their identity provider's own settings, not through this
in-app 2FA flow.
