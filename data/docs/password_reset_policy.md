# Password Reset Policy

## Resetting your password (consumer accounts)

Go to Settings then Security then Reset Password. Enter the email
address associated with your account and a reset link will be sent.
The link expires 24 hours after it is issued. If it expires, request
a new one from the same screen.

## Password requirements

Passwords must be at least 10 characters long and include one number
and one symbol. Passwords cannot match any of your last 5 passwords.

## Locked accounts

After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes.
During this window, a password reset link can still be requested and
will unlock the account immediately once used.

## Note on enterprise SSO accounts

If your organization has enterprise single sign-on enabled, this
consumer password reset flow does not apply to you. See the SSO and
Enterprise Login document for the correct process — resetting a
password here will not affect your SSO login.
