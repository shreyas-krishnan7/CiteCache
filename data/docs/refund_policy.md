# Refund Policy

## Standard refund window

Refunds are available within 14 days of the original charge for any
paid plan. Requests after 14 days are evaluated on a case-by-case
basis and are not guaranteed.

## How to request a refund

Go to Billing then Payment History, select the charge, and click
Request Refund. Refunds are processed to the original payment method
within 5-10 business days.

## Refunds for annual plans

Annual plans are refundable on a prorated basis within the first 30
days of the annual term, after which no refund is issued for the
remainder of the year if you cancel.

## Refunds after a downgrade

Downgrading a plan does not automatically trigger a refund for the
unused portion of the current billing cycle. You will continue to
have access to your previous plan's features until the end of the
cycle you already paid for.
