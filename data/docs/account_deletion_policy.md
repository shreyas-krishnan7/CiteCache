# Account Deletion Policy

## Requesting deletion

Go to Settings then Account then Delete Account. You will be asked to
confirm via a link sent to your registered email address.

## Grace period

Deleted accounts enter a 30-day grace period during which the account
can be restored by logging back in. After 30 days, all data is
permanently and irreversibly deleted.

## What gets deleted

All personal data, uploaded files, and API keys are deleted. Billing
records required for tax and legal compliance are retained separately
for 7 years, but are no longer linked to an active account.

## Deleting an organization account

Deleting an organization account requires confirmation from the
account owner and removes access for all members immediately, though
the same 30-day grace period and data retention rules apply.
