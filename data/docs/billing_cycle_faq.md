# Billing Cycle FAQ

## When am I charged?

Monthly plans are charged on the same calendar day each month as your
original signup date. Annual plans are charged once per year on the
signup anniversary.

## What happens if a payment fails?

We retry a failed payment 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail,
the account is downgraded to the free tier and you will receive an
email with a link to update your payment method and restore access.

## Can I change my billing date?

Billing dates cannot be changed directly, but canceling and
re-subscribing will reset it to the new signup date. Note this may
result in a gap in paid access if not timed carefully.

## Proration when upgrading mid-cycle

Upgrading mid-cycle charges a prorated amount immediately for the
remainder of the current cycle, then bills the full new plan price on
the next regular billing date.
